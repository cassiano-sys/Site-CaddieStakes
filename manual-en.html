Step 1
01

Install the app on your phone

Caddie Stakes Golf isn't in an app store — it installs straight from the link below, like a shortcut. Once that's done, it works just like any other app on your home screen.

Caddie Stakes Golf address
app.caddiestakesgolf.com
Point your phone's camera at the QR code, or type the address into your browser.

📱 iPhone

  1. Open this link (above) in Safari (it has to be Safari)
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner

🤖 Android

  1. Open this link (above) in Chrome
  2. Tap the three dots in the top-right corner
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen"
  4. Confirm
Tip: once installed, the app icon sits on your home screen just like any other app. You won't need to repeat this step again.
Step 2
02

Sign in to the app

The first time, you'll need to create an account with an email and password. After that, the app remembers you.

  • Tap the Caddie Stakes Golf icon on your home screen
  • Already have an account? Enter your email and password and tap "Entrar" ("Sign in")
  • First time? Tap "Criar conta" ("Create account"), then fill in your name, email and password

04

Start a new round

This is where you set up the game: pick the course, who's playing, and the wager format. If you've played with these people before, their names pop up on their own — just tap to fill them in.

  • Choose the course (Graciosa is already set up)
  • Type each player's name — names you've used before show up in a small list, tap one to fill it in
  • Check the Nassau format and the bet amount per point
  • Tap "Iniciar rodada" ("Start round")

Step 5
05

Enter your scores

There are three ways to enter scores in the app. Use whichever is easiest for you — they all lead to the same place.

🎯 Hole by hole

A new hole already shows the par number in gray — tap it and the score is set to par right away, no need to tap + or − first. Then just adjust with the buttons, or tap the number and type a value directly.

⌨️ Quick entry

A grid with all 18 holes on one screen. Tap a hole's box and type the number directly. Faster if you already know the scores by heart.

📷 Scorecard photo

Take a photo of the hand-filled paper scorecard. The app reads the numbers on its own — you just confirm or correct them before saving.

Tip: on the photo review screen, every number stays editable. If a hole gets misread, just tap it and fix it before saving — nothing is lost. To double-check faster without going hole by hole, that same screen shows each player's Front 9, Back 9 and Total right under their name, added up live from what the AI actually read — just match it against the total you added up yourself on the paper card.

Important: the "Cartão" / "Resumo" ("Scorecard" / "Summary") toggle at the top of the screen is always visible — it's what switches between entering scores and seeing the wager result. After hole 18, tap "Resumo" to see who won (Step 6).
Step 6
06

See the result and save the round

After the last hole, the app settles the numbers on its own and shows, front and center, how much each player actually won or lost — that's the figure that decides the day's wager.

  • The screen always opens on "Cartão" — tap "Resumo", at the top, to see the final result
  • Green with a + means that person won that amount; red with a −, that they lost it
  • Scroll a bit to see the front-nine and back-nine breakdown
  • Scroll to the end and tap "💾 Salvar rodada" ("Save round") — without this, the round won't be saved to your history or counted in the group ranking

Step 7
07

Check history and rankings later

Every saved round stays on record. You can look back at any past game, see who's ahead overall, or check your personal track record against a specific player.

  • In the bottom menu, tap "Histórico" ("History") to see past rounds
  • Tap "Ranking" and then "Geral" ("Overall") to see each player's running balance
  • Tap "H2H" for head-to-head — type a player's name to filter just the games against them, and use the time filters (week, month, year) to track your recent form

Step 8
?

Common questions

I forgot my password — now what?

On the sign-in screen, look for the password recovery option (or ask Cassiano to set up a fresh account for you, if you'd rather keep it simple).

I entered a wrong number — does that break the round?

No. Every score can be corrected at any time, even after it's been entered — just tap the number again and change it.

The scorecard photo misread a number

That happens sometimes, especially with tight handwriting. The review screen always shows the numbers before saving — fix whatever you need right there.

Do I need to reinstall it every time?

No — once installed (Step 1), the icon stays on your home screen like any other app.

I got stuck mid-round

Just call Cassiano — he keeps an eye on the app and can help fix anything on the spot, even after a round has already been saved.
